Account Deletion

You can request deletion without signing in.

Request deletion

If you can sign in, use the Account or Profile screen in Hercules Honors Rewards to request deletion. If you cannot sign in, you may use the online request form or call Hercules support and ask to submit a Hercules Honors Rewards account-deletion request.

Open the online deletion request form

Support phone: 1-800-356-5350

What happens next

A dealer deletion removes the account and associated app data once verified. A sales manager deletion closes access immediately and is completed after assigned dealer records are safely reassigned. Hercules aims to complete verified requests within 30 days and will confirm when the process is complete.

Limited records may be retained only where required by law, necessary to resolve a security issue, or needed for an active business record that must first be reassigned.
