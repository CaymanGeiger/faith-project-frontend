Hercules Honors Rewards

Privacy Notice

Effective August 17, 2026
This notice explains how Hercules Industries, Inc. handles information in the Hercules Honors Rewards business app.

Information we collect

We collect account and business details you provide: name, email address, optional phone number, company name, and an internal account identifier. Account credentials are processed as protected password hashes.

To provide the app, we process training preference and onboarding activity, equipment names, model and serial numbers, marketing consultation availability, manager lead notes, and private messages between assigned dealers and sales managers.

How we use and share information

Hercules uses this information to authenticate users, provide dealer training and support, manage the assigned manager relationship, operate app features, secure the service, and meet legal obligations. We do not use this app for advertising, tracking across other companies' apps or websites, or selling personal information.

Information is available to assigned Hercules team members who need it to operate the dealer program and to service providers that host or secure the app for Hercules. If a new outside integration is added, Hercules will update this notice and its app-store disclosures before transferring data.

Retention and deletion

You can correct account details in the app. You can request deletion from the Profile or Account screen, or use the public account-deletion information below. Dealer account data is deleted when the in-app deletion process completes. A sales manager deletion request closes access immediately and is completed after active dealer relationships are reassigned.

Hercules aims to complete verified requests within 30 days and will confirm when the process is complete. Hercules may retain limited information only when required by law, necessary to resolve a security issue, or needed for an active business record that must first be reassigned.

Security and children

The production service uses authenticated, encrypted connections and limits access to authorized business users. The app is intended for adult dealers and Hercules personnel; it is not directed to children.

Contact Hercules

For privacy questions, account assistance, or a report about content in the app, call 1-800-356-5350 or visit herculesindustries.com.
